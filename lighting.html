Lighting & tech

Colton has worked professionally as a lighting designer and operator for more than five years. His approach is highly visual but never merely decorative: lighting is a way to direct attention, tell time, reveal a feeling, and help a production become a world.

From theatre to choral and commercial music, he is interested in the alchemy of a live room—the equipment, preparation, timing, and intuition that make a cue land.
