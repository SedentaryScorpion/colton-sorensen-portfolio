The throughline

One curious creative life.

There’s no tidy way to explain a creative life. Colton’s has included singing into a microphone, calling lighting cues, building a character from the ground up, arranging music for a room full of voices, and making maps for worlds that don’t exist—yet.

It all belongs to the same practice: noticing what a moment needs, then giving it shape. This is where the experiments, obsessions, and favorite projects live.

The teaching practice

Make room
for everybody.

“The more parts of the process we understand, the more fully we can make something together.”

Teaching, for Colton, is an invitation to explore. Through music and theatre, he gives students a wider view of the whole production—and the confidence to find where they belong in it.
