The voice room

Voice performance asks for the smallest, most exact choices. A shift in breath, rhythm, or tone can create a person the listener sees without ever seeing them.

Colton’s voice work includes character monologues, showcase material, and original audio drama. He brings a performer’s emotional specificity to the mic—while making room for the strange, fun, dramatic edges of a new character.
