Theatre & music education

The philosophy

Colton believes artistic education should feel like an invitation, not a gate. He values the student who wants to act, the student who wants to know how the microphone works, and the student who has not found their place in the process yet.

His teaching is built around exposure, connection, and collaboration. Students learn more deeply when they understand the wider ecosystem of a production—and when the room makes it safe to be curious.

01
