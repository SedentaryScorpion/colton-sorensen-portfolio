Music, arrangement & collaboration

The music room

Music is a place where Colton gets to build from the smallest possible material: a breath, a rhythm, a line. A Southern Virginia University alumnus and former member of Accolade, Chamber Singers, and Men’s Chorus, he brings the same curiosity to commercial music, choral work, and vocal percussion.

As an arranger and conductor, he loves the point where individual voices learn to trust each other—and suddenly become a whole.
