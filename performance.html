Theatre & musical theatre

The work

Colton approaches performance from the inside out: find the pulse of the character, make a specific choice, and let the audience see someone—not a performance of someone.

He is especially at home in theatrical stories with bite, warmth, and a little theatricality. The result is work that can be full-throated and funny one moment, then unexpectedly precise the next.
